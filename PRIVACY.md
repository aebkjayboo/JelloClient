# Jello — Privacy Policy

**Owner:** Sun™
**Product:** Jello Client
**Effective:** 6 September 2026

This policy explains what Jello collects, why, and what is done with it. It is written
to be read, not to hide behind. The Terms of Service that sit alongside it are kept
current in the **#information** channel of the Sun Discord server:
<https://discord.gg/tpnP9NNBap>

By installing or using Jello, you accept this policy as it stands at the time you use it.

---

## In short

- Jello is **in contact with Sun's servers** while it runs.
- Your **hardware ID (HWID) is hashed** before it is used — Sun stores the hash, not your
  raw hardware serial numbers.
- Your **IP address and technical identifiers are used** to run the service and to enforce
  the rules — including detecting rule-breakers and applying blacklists.
- Jello does **not** sell your data.

---

## What is collected

- **A hashed hardware identifier (HWID).** A stable value derived from your device is
  passed through a one-way hash. The hash is what identifies a device for licensing and
  enforcement; the original hardware values are not stored.
- **Your IP address.** Seen whenever the software contacts Sun's servers, as it is for any
  online service.
- **Technical identifiers.** The values needed to operate the service, tie a session to an
  account or device, and enforce the Terms.

Jello does not ask for, and this policy does not cover, your Roblox password. Where the
software needs your existing Roblox login on your own machine for a specific action, that
is described where the action is offered.

## Why it is collected, and how it is used

- **To run the service** — to let your device talk to Sun's servers and function as
  intended.
- **To enforce the Terms of Service** — to detect abuse and rule-breaking, and to apply
  **blacklists** against devices, identifiers, or IP addresses that break the rules.
- **For security** — to protect the service and its users from abuse.

Your identifiers and IP address may be used to associate rule-breaking with a device or
person and to keep a blacklisted party out. This is the core reason these identifiers are
processed, and using Jello means accepting it.

## What is not done

- Your data is **not sold**.
- It is **not shared** with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, to
  enforce the Terms, or where required by law.
- Raw hardware serials are **not stored** — only the hash.

## Retention

Identifiers and enforcement records are kept for as long as they are needed to operate the
service and to keep enforcement (bans and blacklists) effective. Records tied to a
rule-breaking party may be kept for as long as that enforcement stands.

## Your choices

Using Jello is voluntary. If you do not accept this policy or the Terms of Service, do not
install or use the software. Questions and requests can be raised in the **#information**
channel of the Sun Discord server.

## Changes

This policy and the Terms of Service are developed on an ongoing basis and are kept current
in **#information**. Continued use of Jello after a change means you accept the updated
version.
